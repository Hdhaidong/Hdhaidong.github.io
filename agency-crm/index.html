Agency CRM

The customer-relationship spine under agency operations and brand management — built for how account teams actually work.

Type: CRM system For: Agency & brand operations Status: Private product line
← Back to portfolio

What it manages

Generic CRMs track contacts. This one tracks the work between contacts.

Client pipelines

Where every engagement stands — from prospect to active account to renewal decision — staged the way agency business actually moves.

Contact history

Every meaningful interaction in one timeline per client, so whoever picks up the account next inherits the full context.

Campaign touchpoints

Links relationship events to the campaign work happening in parallel — the CRM knows what the delivery team is doing.

Renewal signals

Early-warning indicators ahead of renewal windows: engagement dips, escalated issues, scope drift — surfaced before they become churn.

Positioning

The relationship layer of the agency stack.

Agency CRM sits beneath insightmarketplac's operations partitions: the platform runs the campaigns, the CRM keeps the client. Together they cover the two things an agency can't afford to lose track of — the work and the relationship.

Private build — introduced by concept only. Source code and client data are not published.
