PI · Pocket Intelligence

A pocketable edge-AI companion — intelligence that lives next to you, not in someone else's cloud.

Type: Edge device Family: Deck hardware line Status: Private product line
← Back to portfolio

Design principles

The Deck family's privacy architecture, scaled down to something you carry.

Local-first inference

Small-model reasoning runs on the device. Personal context never needs a round-trip to a server to be useful.

Physical consent control

Actions that matter — sharing, syncing, connecting — answer to a physical control you press, in the Approve-gate tradition of the Deck line.

Pull-out private storage

Your data lives on a removable encrypted card. Pocket the card and the intelligence is yours, alone — leave it and the device knows nothing.

Companion, not phone

A single-purpose object: glanceable, patient, and quiet. It does one relationship — you and your assistant — without the attention economy attached.

Architecture

A vertical stack of privacy — the closer data gets to leaving the device, the more physical the gate.

LayerDesign
Interaction surfaceE-ink glance, status ring, physical Approve control — the Deck line's consent discipline at pocket scale
Edge inferenceSmall-model reasoning on local silicon; personal context stays useful without a server round-trip
Private storageRemovable encrypted cartridge, key bound to card hardware — pocket the card and the device is blank
Consent gateSharing, syncing and connecting answer to a hardware press, not a dialog box
Sync boundaryAn external link exists only as an opt-in, and only opens after approval
LineageSame principles as the open-source OpenWorker Deck, scaled from gateway to pocket
DisclosurePrinciple-level description — hardware designs and firmware stay unpublished

Positioning

Where the Deck line started heading, in pocket form.

The open-source Grok Deck and OpenWorker Deck explore the same principles — physical authorization, data sovereignty, compute where it serves you — at gateway scale. PI is the private exploration of what those ideas become when the whole device fits in a pocket.

Private concept — architecture described at principle level. Hardware designs and firmware are not published.
