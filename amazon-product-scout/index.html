Amazon Product Scout

Niche research, product development and competitor tracking — a business agent that does the research legwork of Amazon selling.

Type: Business agent Built on: OpenWorker Focus: Amazon e-commerce
GitHub repository → More projects

What it does

One agent covering the full research loop that sellers repeat by hand every week.

Niche discovery

Scans categories for under-served niches — demand signals, review gaps, price-band openings — and ranks the opportunities worth a deeper look.

Competitor tracking

Watches the players in your shortlist: price moves, listing changes, review velocity. New developments surface as digestible briefs, not raw pages.

Listing analysis

Breaks down competing listings — title structure, keyword coverage, image strategy — into concrete actions for your own product page.

Product development pipeline

Carries an opportunity from market scan to a development brief: what to build, for whom, at what price, against which incumbents.

Why an agent, not a dashboard

Dashboards wait for questions. A scout goes looking.

Amazon Product Scout is built as an OpenWorker coworker: it runs continuous research passes in the background, decides what changed and what matters, and reports findings — the way a junior analyst would after a morning of reading. The human stays on the decisions: which niche to enter, which product to build.
